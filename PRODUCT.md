# Taskbook Product

Taskbook is a Nextcloud app for managing tasks, plans, and notes in one simple
daily workspace. It brings quick capture, a chronological daily log, and
lightweight organization together in a calm, Nextcloud-native interface.

It is task, plan, and note oriented; keyboard-friendly; and designed to keep
today's complete record visible, including completed tasks. It is not intended
to become complex enterprise project-management software, impose mandatory
categorization, or automatically interpret or prioritize a user's work.

Taskbook is inspired by concepts from the Bullet Journal® method, but does not
attempt to reproduce that method. Taskbook is an independent application and is
not affiliated with or endorsed by Bullet Journal or Lightcage LLC.
